FROM CITY TO PAPER

Explore. Capture. Export.

  1. Click in the city and explore using its normal controls and transport.
  2. Click Capture view. The city pauses while you prepare your image.
  3. Choose your paper, ink and export mode. The preview shows what will be saved.
  4. Save the SVG, then choose Return to city to carry on from that spot.

Use Printer for filled characters and the full scene. Use Pen plotter for visible character contours without background strokes. Colour plotting uses six pen colours.

This is a separate city session. It does not take over a view already open on the original website. The original city interface and capture controls are excluded from the export.

Use City versions to switch saved releases or add a city URL. Switching restarts the city; SVGs you have saved are unaffected.
