ASCII CITY CAPTURE 1.2.1 INSTALLER — 29 OCTOBER 2026

Four installer policy tests pass. They check a fixed /Applications destination,
non-relocatable app placement, full bundle upgrade, newer-version protection,
strict bundle identity, correct version and Apple silicon/macOS requirements.
These tests were introduced before the implementation.

The package was created with Apple's pkgbuild and productbuild. Its full payload
was independently extracted with pkgutil. Every file in the extracted app was
compared to the original signed 1.2.1 app. The app is the sole installation payload.
Package metadata specifies /Applications, root authorisation, no relocation,
strict identity and upgrade replacement. No preinstall or postinstall scripts
are present. No user data folder is included in the payload.

The distribution limits installation to the local system domain, Apple silicon
and macOS 12 or later. Welcome and completion pages explain installation and
opening the app from Applications. No app is launched from a privileged installer
script; the user opens the app normally after installation.

The extracted app passed its native self-test, its ad-hoc signature verification
and its separate-runtime validation of both included city releases. The original
published ZIP's app was compared byte-for-byte with the installer source bundle.
The final ZIP was checked for CRC integrity and exact package preservation.

The installer package is unsigned and not Apple-notarised. The app itself has
its existing ad-hoc signature. The actual installation into /Applications and
an interactive Installer/Chrome launch were not performed in this session.
The package was inspected and validated in temporary directories only.
